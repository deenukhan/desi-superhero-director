## 1. Model facts (Seedance 2.5, as of late 2026; re-check if the platform shows different options)
| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Duration | 4–30 s, whole seconds. Use **7 s** for a 2–3 shot beat, 5 s for a single wow |
| Resolution | 480p, 720p, 1080p (4K on some platforms) |
| Aspect | 16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 4:3, 3:4, 21:9 |
| References | Up to about 30 images (image references); character sheets work best |
| Audio | Generated by default (ambience, SFX, sometimes music). **If a song will be added in-app, write "no music, no singing, no dialogue" and ask only for SFX**, otherwise the generated score fights the song. Only SFX-only films keep a generated score |
| Multi-shot | Writing timed "Shot 1 (0 to 2 sec) … Shot 2 …" produces hard cuts inside one generation |

## 2. Prompt rules and template
- **One paragraph, no line breaks.** Some platforms submit on Enter. Keep it **≤ 350 words**; longer prompts get partly ignored.
- **Look first, then identity lock, then shots, then audio, then the "no" line.**
- **Identity lock:** map every reference to a role, and describe each character in one line by name and look, so it survives a platform with a different reference syntax. Add the hero build lock ("sturdy broad build, not lean") and the mask state ("mask on in every shot, mouth and beard visible").
- **Shots:** 2–3 per 7 s, each with its time range, **one** camera move, and the events (1 per single-shot clip, ≤ 2 per shot otherwise). Speed ramps go one way only: "opening in slow motion … snap to full speed as …".
- **Start each shot already in motion** ("the hero is already airborne") so the model doesn't spend frames on wind-up or ground contact, which garbles.
- **Name big, simple props.** Spend words on objects and places, not on grade adjectives.
- **The "no" line:** "No text, no logos; the only emblem is <the homemade emblem>." Add "Every person has exactly two arms and two legs. Exactly three shots, two hard cuts."
- **Never write official superhero or villain names** ("Batman", "Joker", "Bane"…) in a prompt. Describe the archetype instead. IP filters and moderation reject them.

**Template**
```
Highly realistic cinematic live-action Indian action-comedy <location> at <time of day>: <light>, <3–5 concrete desi objects>, anamorphic lenses, <grade>, film grain. Identity lock: the masked hero is @image1 (<face source>), sturdy broad build, mid-30s, not lean, in the costume from @image2: <8-word costume summary>, mouth and beard visible, never removed. <Name>, the <look>, is @image3. … Do not redesign any face, body or clothing. Every person has exactly two arms and two legs. Exactly three shots, two hard cuts. Shot 1 (0 to 2 sec): <framing>, <one camera move>, <speed>. <events>. Shot 2 (2 to 4.5 sec): … Shot 3 (4.5 to 7 sec): … Hold. Audio: <ambience>, <hits>, <SFX>. Grunts only, no dialogue. No text, no logos; the only emblem is <homemade emblem>.
```

## 3. A worked example (original hero, rendered successfully)
> Highly realistic cinematic live-action Indian action-comedy in a red-earth wrestling pit under a giant banyan tree in a monsoon storm: slate-grey daylight, steady heavy rain, lightning in the sky, thick mud, anamorphic lenses, cold grey-green grade, film grain. No other people. Identity lock: the masked hero is @image1 (character sheet face and beard), sturdy broad build, mid-30s, not lean, in the costume from @image2 worn as in @image3: black knit hood mask to the nose, tall crooked ears, mouth and beard visible, never removed. The horned Indian giant is @image4: rust-red iron helmet, long cream bull horns, brass nose ring, bare muscular chest, a head taller than the hero; helmet never off; only he has horns. Do not redesign any face, body or clothing. Every person has exactly two arms and two legs. Exactly three shots, two hard cuts. Shot 1 (0 to 2 sec): Fast crash zoom from medium to close on the giant, real time. He paws the mud like a bull, lowers his horns, snorts, and charges. Shot 2 (2 to 4.5 sec): Low side angle at mud level, locked off, opening in slow motion. The hero holds his cape out like a matador, the charging giant inches from it, raindrops frozen mid-air. Snap to full speed: the giant thunders through the empty cape as the hero sidesteps. Shot 3 (4.5 to 7 sec): Low angle up into the rain, slight push-in, opening in slow motion. The hero has just flipped the giant over his shoulder: the giant hangs upside down above him in a lightning flash. Snap to full speed: the giant lands flat on his back in soft mud, unhurt, and a mud wave drenches the hero; only his eyes blink through the mask. Hold. Audio: pounding rain, thunder, a bull snort, a cape snap, a bass hit, a huge mud splash, a dazed groan. Grunts and roars only, no dialogue. No text, no logos; the only emblem is the crooked hand-cut white cloth bat on his kurta, never a yellow oval.

## 4. Platform notes
| Platform | References in the prompt | Cost and limits (observed late 2026) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Higgsfield** (web or MCP) | `@image1…@imageN` in upload order. Saved "Elements" can be injected by ID via the MCP | 7 s Seedance 2.5: 480p ≈ 21 credits, 720p ≈ 49, 1080p ≈ 112. Max 4 jobs at once | Has **draft mode**: a 480p draft (≈ 21) can be finalized at 1080p within 7 days (≈ 112) with the same motion. Image sheets ≈ 1–2 credits |
| **Runway** (web) | Upload images, then type `@Image 1` and press Enter to turn each into a chip; check every chip rendered | Unlimited plans: free but **one job at a time account-wide**, and relaxed queues can wait minutes to hours | Draft mode only in credits mode. Duration slider (set 7 s). Audio toggle under settings |
| **Others** (Kling, Veo, MiniMax…) | Follow the platform's syntax; keep the same prompt structure | Varies | Kling multi-shot needs a start frame for reference lock on some versions |

**With an MCP connected** (Higgsfield, Magnific, OpenArt…):
1. Estimate the cost first and tell the user.
2. Upload the reference images.
3. Generate the sheets.
4. Check them.
5. Generate 480p drafts in a batch, respecting the concurrency limit.
6. Download, frame-check, then finalize the approved ones.

**Without one:** output a run sheet per clip: upload order (file names), settings (model, aspect, resolution, duration, audio), and the prompt in a code block.

## 5. Draft → check → finalize
1. Generate every clip as a **480p draft**, or on Unlimited, directly at the target resolution.
2. Make a frame sheet per clip (`scripts/frame_sheet.sh clip.mp4`) and check it:
   - Is the hero on-model and masked as agreed?
   - Does each shot's action read?
   - No extra limbs, no morphing faces?
   - Is the emblem still homemade, not a famous logo?
   - Nothing unsafe?
3. Reroll a failed clip once with a **simpler** shot. Then change the idea, not the seed.
4. Finalize only the approved drafts at 1080p.
5. Join and deliver (`scripts/join_clips.sh`).

## 6. When a generation fails
| Symptom | Fix |
|---|---|
| Image or video job fails with a generic error | Usually moderation. Soften words like "villain", "vigilante" or "weapon"; remove any official character name; retry on another model |
| Hero face drifts or changes age or build | Add the build lock; lead with the face reference; keep the face at medium distance or masked; avoid extreme close-ups of the face |
| Costume emblem turns into the official logo | Make the sheet's emblem more broken: one wing short, a notch, crooked stitches; say "never a yellow oval" |
| Choreography garbles (merged bodies) | Fewer events, start mid-action, one contact per shot, side-on framing |
| The reveal is too small | Move to a closer framing or let the toy cause something bigger than the hero |
| Unwanted speech | "Grunts only, no dialogue" + "wordless roar"; mute and use SFX in the edit |
