## 1. Prompt conventions

- **"Exactly N shots, N−1 hard cuts"**, each shot with its own time range ("Shot 2 (2 to 4.5 sec)").
- **Shots of 2–3.5 s** inside a gen; 2–3 shots per 7 s, 1–2 per 5 s.
- **Events:** an *event* is a change in what a subject does (swings, ducks, lands, falls). Camera moves and lighting don't count. **1 event in a single-shot wow clip; ≤ 2 per shot in a multi-shot gen; 4 is the hard cap.**
- **One camera move per shot.**
- **Frame 0 is a complete, striking image** (mid-action, never black or empty). The reveal itself lands on the drop.
- **Start mid-action to skip hard contact:** "already airborne above the bench", "already mid-swing". Foot-to-object contact and grabs are where bodies garble.
- **No tiny wides at 480p/720p:** bodies fill at least half the frame height.
- **One identity-lock line per reference**, then: "Do not redesign any face, body or clothing. Every person has exactly two arms and two legs."
- **End with:** "Hold." for a held button, then the audio line and the "no text, no logos" line.
- **≤ 350 words, one paragraph** (see `seedance-and-generation.md`).

## 2. Camera moves

| Move | Purpose | Seedance phrasing |
|---|---|---|
| **Slow push-in** | Resolve, menace, a stare, the end | "Close-up, slow push-in, [hero] turns his eyes to the lens, deadpan." |
| **Pull-out reveal** | Show what he's standing on / up against | "Starting on [hero]'s face, slow pull-back reveals he is standing on [huge thing]." |
| **Extreme low angle** | Heroic scale for trivial acts | "Extreme low angle from the ground, [hero] silhouetted against a low golden sun, cape spread." |
| **Tilt-up from the feet** | Hero or ally reveal | "Start on worn rubber chappals in the dust, slow tilt up past the gamchha belt and cape to the masked face." |
| **Half-orbit (180°)** | Fight energy, a face-off | "Continuous half-orbit around the two fighters at waist height." (Safer than a full 360°.) |
| **Top-down / god's-eye** | Climax escalation, geography | "Top-down overhead shot, camera slowly rotates above the brawl in the circle of light." |
| **Crane down / up** | Arrival or aftermath | "Slow crane down to ground level as the dust settles." |
| **Lateral dolly** | Corridors, chases, wipes through cloth | "Fast lateral dolly along the narrow brick corridor, left to right." |
| **Tracking ahead of a charge** | The toy or a chase coming at us | "Camera tracks backward low ahead of the charging [toy], dust kicking up." |
| **Handheld circling** | Crowd chaos, one-take brawls | "Handheld shot circling the two fighters inside the dancing crowd." |
| **Drone sweep** | Opening a big location | "Fast drone shot sweeping down the ghat steps toward [hero]." |
| **Whip-pan** | Energy, hidden cuts | "Fast whip-pan that settles on [subject]." |
| **Crash zoom (medium → close)** | A villain's charge, a gag beat | "Fast crash zoom from medium to close on [villain]." (Never start from a tiny wide.) |
| **Long-lens compression** | Stacks hero and danger | "Long lens, [hero] small in front, [toy] huge behind him, both sharp." |
| **Locked-off** | Comedy timing; the hold is the joke | "Locked-off medium shot, real time." |
| **Rack focus** | Plant → payoff, frame-in-frame | "Rack focus from [prop] in the foreground to [subject] behind." |
| **Walk into lens** | Endings, menace | "[Hero] walks straight toward camera until his chest fills the frame." |

## 3. Framings

| Framing | Purpose | Seedance phrasing |
|---|---|---|
| **ECU prop insert (0.5–1 s)** | Punctuation, plant/payoff, suit-up | "Extreme close-up macro of [prop] in [hand], shallow depth of field, slow push-in, real time." |
| **ECU eyes** | Resolve; power ignition | "Extreme close-up of [hero]'s eyes through the mask holes, dust in the backlight." |
| **Medium two-shot** | Fights, comic payoffs | "Medium two-shot at eye level, [A] left, [B] right." |
| **Shot-reverse-shot** | Glance → stare → deflation | Shot A: "over-the-shoulder of [group] passing"; Shot B: "close-up, [hero] turns his head slowly to stare, deadpan." |
| **Foreground occlusion** | Villain intro | "Medium close-up, [villain] half-hidden behind [hanging chilli garlands] under a bare bulb, slow push-in, he does not move." |
| **Hide-and-pop** | Silent comedy | "Locked-off medium shot of [object] in the foreground; the masked head slowly rises from behind it and peeks." |
| **Frame-in-frame** | Brands the world | "Shot through the spokes of a hanging cycle wheel, [action] framed inside the rim." |
| **POV of the hidden** | Puts us with the witness | "Ground-level shot through the legs of a plastic stool, the fight in soft focus beyond." |
| **Calm front / chaos behind** | Single-shot wow | "Vertical frame, long lens, [hero] small in the lower third sitting calmly; top two-thirds empty; at 2 seconds [toy] bursts into the empty space behind him in slow motion; he never turns." |
| **Backlit doorway / smoke** | Entrance on the drop | "Wide, backlit doorway, [character] steps out of thick white smoke toward camera, cloth swirling, opening in slow motion." |
| **Silhouette against the sun** | Cover frame, mid-air peak | "Extreme low angle into the low sun, [hero] already airborne in silhouette, cape spread like wings." |
| **Symmetrical centred** | Rebirth, a new costume, a corridor | "Centred symmetrical wide, [character] stands framed in a doorway, still." |
| **Lit circle** | Readable cheap fights | "Night, a single white streetlight pool on sand, darkness around, the fight inside the circle." |
| **Village tableau (1 s)** | Localises a chase | "Static medium shot, a barber freezes mid-shave as [toy] thunders past behind him." |
| **Product ECU slide** | Sponsor beat | "Slow lateral slide macro across [product] on [hero's workbench], clean dusk light." |

## 4. Transitions

| Transition | Use | How |
|---|---|---|
| **Hard cut on the beat** | Default | Cut on a downbeat or on motion |
| **Smash cut** | Calm → chaos; KO bursts | Quiet frame straight into the loudest frame |
| **Whip wipe** | Energy, scene change | "Fast whip-pan blur across a brick wall"; cut inside the blur |
| **Foreground wipe** | Comic interruption | "A tractor with a hay trolley crosses the foreground left to right, wiping the frame." |
| **Match cut** | Time jump, flashback | Match a shape: a spinning cycle wheel → a spinning chakki stone, re-graded |
| **Hidden cut** | Fake a long take from two gens | End gen 1 and start gen 2 inside the same whip-pan blur |
| **Flash insert (0.3–0.5 s)** | Recall a plant or earlier move | One frame-accurate insert from an earlier clip; an edit, not a new gen |
| **Cut to black** | Only at the very end | After a held stare; never on frame 1 |

## 5. Reveal orders

**Power reveal (7 steps, ≈ 8–12 s)**
1. **Provocation** (the villain lunges) → **startle CU**: the hero looks at his own hands.
2. **Silence dip** (0.5–2 s).
3. **Source ECU ignites** (eyes glow, palms crackle, the weapon hums).
4. **Effect CU** leaving frame (a beam, a gust, a shockwave in the dust).
5. **Effect wide:** it hits a landmark (a water tank, a hoarding, a haystack). Never a person.
6. **Consequence wide**, held 2–3 s (it topples, bursts, scatters).
7. **Afterglow hold:** the hero in settling dust as the glow fades (3–5 s).

**Toy reveal in parts (≈ 10–13 s). Never show it whole before the drop.**
1. ECU of one part (an eye, a headlamp, a bell) with a slow push.
2. Full body in god-rays (a barn, a garage, a dust storm), walking toward camera.
3. **The mount / arrival on the drop**, silhouette against the sky, witnesses cheering on that exact frame.
4. The head or front pushing through foreground leaves or hanging cloth.
5. Hooves or wheels kicking dust, low tracking.
6. A frontal charge to the lens.
7. ECU of the **branding ornament**.

*Sequel variant:* it bursts through a wall of drying saris, hay bales or a paper banner → ornament → wheels/hooves → mount → charge. Never through fire.

## 6. Fight staging

| Pattern | Structure | Use for |
|---|---|---|
| **Lit-circle theatre** | A fixed wide master + slow-mo ECU impact inserts + bystander cutaways | Comedy fights, 15–60 s films |
| **Coverage ladder** | Static wides → slow-mo impact ECUs → half-orbit → speed-ramped throw → god's-eye overhead | The climax of a 90–120 s episode |
| **One-take brawl + bursts** | One 10–14 s handheld take (he dispatches 2–3 goons in turn), then **3–4 smash cuts of 0.6–0.75 s**, then a chase | The centrepiece of a 150 s+ event |
| **Slow-mo impact ECU** | "Extreme close-up, opening in slow motion, a fist lands on [villain]'s cheek, a halo of powder bursts, snap to full speed." | Selling any hit without gore |
| **Bystander cutaway** | Cut to the indifferent chore-doer 2–3 times | The punchline of any fight |
| **Short CU exchanges** | 2–4 shots per action, POV fists, a held face-off | Fights in water, mud, rain, crowds (hides limbs) |
| **Two-shot KO** | Cause CU (slow-mo) → effect WS (he flies into soft hay) | Teasers, wow clips |
| **Reaction chain** | 4 separate CU reactions, 0.4–0.6 s each | "Oh no" before a stunt |

**Gen-by-gen recipe for a 15–30 s fight (3–5 gens of 7 s)**

| Gen | Shots |
|---|---|
| 1 Set-up | Wide face-off in the lit circle (or drone in) · villain's gag-property ECU · hero cracks knuckles (slow push) |
| 2 Round 1 | Villain's swing, hero ducks (slow from frame 0, snap) · slow-mo impact ECU · bystander cutaway |
| 3 Reversal | The dirty trick (chilli, oil) · the hero's counter turns it back · reaction chain |
| 4 Climax | Half-orbit exchange · speed-ramped throw into something soft (hay, suds, mud) |
| 5 Button | Villain dazed but fine · hero's held stare or humiliated button · bystander still at his chore |

**Fight safety:** every fall lands in something soft (hay, suds, mud, brick dust); everyone moves after a hit (dazed, groaning, even snoring); no blood, no realistic injury, no weapon used on a person's head in close-up; no fire near people; the elder is never thrown; the child only cheers.

## 7. One-way speed ramps

- **Start slow at frame 0, snap to full speed once.** Write: "opening in slow motion… snap to full speed as [impact]."
- **Never full → slow → full** inside one shot; the model smears it.
- Frame 0 should be the **most dramatic frame** of the shot (the lathi inches from the mask, the hero already mid-air), so the slow opening doubles as the scroll-stop.
- Real time is fine for comic holds: "real time… Hold."
- If the model ignores the ramp, the shot must still work at full speed. Treat slow motion as garnish, not structure.

## 8. Hiding AI weaknesses

| Weakness | Hide it with |
|---|---|
| Limb tangles in fights | Short shots; dust, rain, backlight; one contact point per shot; side-on framing |
| Tiny props vanish | Make props big (a kadhai, not a coin) or give them their own ECU |
| Face drift | Masks with a strong anchor; the face at medium distance; few faces per gen (≤ 4) |
| Morphing at the end of a clip | Cut before the last second; end on a held button |
| Text and signage garble | No text in prompts; titles go on in the edit |
| Crowd faces sharpen and multiply | "Crowd in soft focus"; "no other people" when you don't need them |

---

## 9. Anti-clone rule

Compositions are free to use; specifics are not. Any output that borrows a format from a famous post (the calm sitter with a huge arrival behind, the parts-reveal of an animal vehicle, a monochrome ident) must change **the hero, the toy, the setting, the music and the gag type**. Never copy another creator's character, costume, plot, cover or ident.
