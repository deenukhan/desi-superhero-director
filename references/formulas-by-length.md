# Formulas by length

Beat sheets in seconds for every length, from a 5 s wow clip to a 3-minute event. Use the user's **stated length** first, then the verb ("clip", "reel", "episode", "film") only when no length is given.

**Contents**
1. How to read these sheets (units, gens, two ways to spend a gen)
2. Quick table
3. A: 5–8 s single wow
4. B: 15–30 s micro-story
5. C: 45–60 s vignette or teaser (with the drop defined)
6. D: 90–120 s episode (the sweet spot)
7. E: 150–200 s event (song change, offer parts)
8. Required slots in every format: hook, build, drop, escalation ladder, button
9. Tie-breaks and edge cases

---

## 1. How to read these sheets

- **Gen** = one Seedance generation of **5–7 s**. A 7 s gen holds 2–3 timed shots with hard cuts; a 5 s gen holds 1–2. Add **30–50% for rerolls**, and draft at 480p before any 1080p final.
- **Percentages** are the template. The real drop time comes from the user's song (see `music-and-edit.md`). **Fit the edit to the song, never the song to the template.**
- **Average shot length** in the top-performing desi superhero series sat at a median of **1.2–2.1 s**. The link between faster cutting and more likes is directional, not strong: the one clearly slow episode (median 3 s) was also the only early dip. Keep the median **≤ 2 s** and add beats, not longer holds, when a film gets longer.

**Two ways to spend a gen** (pick per film and say which):

| Mode | How | Average shot | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Beat clips** | Each 7 s gen is a complete beat (setup → escalation → payoff, held comic button) and is used almost whole | about 2.3 s | 15–60 s films, fight compilations, anthology episodes. Cheapest per second |
| **Coverage** | Each gen gives 2–3 shots; keep the best 1–2 s of each | 1.2–2 s | 90 s+ episodes, chases, montages, anything cut tight to a song |

## 3. A: 5–8 s single wow

**What the user wants:** one shareable moving image. **Not** a squeezed story.

**Rules**
- A **"5 s" request becomes one 5 s generation.** An 8 s request becomes one 8 s generation. Don't generate 7 s and trim unless the user asks.
- **One event, one long hold.** The event is a change in what the subject does; camera moves don't count.
- **The wow fills ≥ 40% of the frame** or is bigger than the hero. A hand-sized prop far away is not a wow; it reads as a dot on a phone.
- **Frame 0 is a complete, striking image** (never black, never empty). The calm is *alive*: mask adjust, cloth in wind, steam, a slow push-in.
- The hero never reacts to the wow. Deadpan is the joke.
- End **before** the model starts to morph. Watch the last second of every draft.

**Beat sheet**

| Beat | 5 s clip | 8 s clip | % |
|---|---|---|---|
| Alive calm: hero small in the lower third, the top two-thirds empty and "waiting" | 0.0–1.6 | 0.0–2.6 | 0–33% |
| Cue + dip: one gesture (fist up, a whistle, a cup set down); ambience drops | 1.6–2.0 | 2.6–3.2 | 33–40% |
| **Reveal on the hit:** the toy, power or entrance bursts in behind or beside him | **2.0** | **3.2** | **40%** |
| Hold the image in slow motion; hero still deadpan, one micro-action (folds arms) | 2.0–4.5 | 3.2–7.2 | 40–90% |
| Button: a tiny final action or clean end frame | 4.5–5.0 | 7.2–8.0 | 90–100% |

- **Shots:** 1 (best). A 3-shot variant inside one gen is allowed (prop ECU → reveal → reaction), still with one event.
- **Music:** one hit after a dip. Choose the song's start point so its hit lands at 40% (`music-and-edit.md` §3). Without a song, use the SFX-only recipe (`music-and-edit.md` §6).
- **Build / escalation:** the calm phase *is* the build. No ladder: one rung only.

**Two variants. Say which one you are making.**

| Variant | When | What to show |
|---|---|---|
| **Spin-off of a known element** | Something from an earlier episode broke out (fans are naming it in comments) | That element arriving huge behind the calm hero; caption names it. In one top-performing series, a 9 s one-shot meme of the breakout toy got **43% of the parent episode's likes from 1 generation** |
| **Cold debut** | A brand-new hero nobody knows yet | The archetype's silhouette readable in 0.5 s + the biggest thing in his world (an animal, a vehicle, a storm of objects). Warn the user: a cold debut won't hit spin-off numbers, because nothing is pre-sold |

Other single-wow compositions: a backlit doorway or smoke entrance; a slow tilt-up from chappals to mask; a calm stare as chaos arrives behind; the power-reveal triplet (eyes ignite → beam leaves frame → effect lands). Change the specifics every time (`shot-grammar.md` §7).

## 4. B: 15–30 s micro-story

**30 s beat sheet**

| Time | % | Beat | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–2 | 0–7% | **Cold hook:** the strongest image, or the hero doing a chore in costume | Works for someone who has never seen the series |
| 2–6 | 7–20% | **Stakes in one insert** (a missing-goat poster, a torn admit card, a broken hand pump) | Personal, not trivial (`story-and-series.md` §3) |
| 6–8.5 | 20–28% | **Inciting act** by the villain or the problem | |
| 8.5–10.5 | 28–35% | **Build:** 2 suit-up ECUs (knot tied, mask pulled down) + 1 tease of the reveal | Required slot |
| 10.5–11 | 35–37% | Dip / cue | Near-silence, or an SFX-only dip |
| **11** | **37%** | **Reveal on the drop** (toy, hero, ally) | |
| 11–22 | 37–73% | **Escalation ladder, 2 rungs:** rung 1 on the ground, rung 2 bigger (height, a vehicle, water); 1 slow-mo impact ECU | 1 s shots |
| 22–26 | 73–87% | Payoff + anticlimax gag | |
| 26–30 | 87–100% | **Button:** held stare, a gag, or "To Be Continued" (ident ≤ 3 s) | |

**15 s version:** hook 0–1.5 · stakes + inciting in one shot 1.5–3.5 · build (1 ECU) 3.5–4.5 · dip 4.5–5 · **reveal at 5 (33%)** · one rung of action + an impact ECU 5–11 · payoff and button 11–15.

- **Shots:** 14–20 at 30 s; 8–10 at 15 s. **Average shot:** 1.5–2 s (beat-clip mode: about 2.3 s).
- **Gens:** 5–7 at 30 s (4–5 in beat-clip mode); 3–4 at 15 s.
- **Music map:** intro under the hook and stakes → build under the suit-up → **drop = reveal** → chorus to the end. Hard-cut the biggest visual change on a chorus downbeat.
- **Text:** a title card ≤ 2 s on the hook image; an optional end card.

## 5. C: 45–60 s vignette or teaser

**The drop, defined:** the moment the song goes from build to full energy (the beat drop or the first chorus hit). In C it carries the **main reveal** (the power, the toy or the hero in full) at **40–50% of runtime**, default **45%** (27 s of a 60 s film, 21 s of a 45 s film). A **second hit** (a chorus downbeat) at **75–80%** carries the top rung of the ladder or the button.

**60 s beat sheet**

| Time | % | Beat | Song section |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3 | 0–5% | **Hook:** a mysterious ECU ritual (feet, hands, the mask going on) or an incongruity gag | Intro |
| 3–15 | 5–25% | **Setup:** a "powers" montage of everyday acts in ECU inserts; **plant** one prop | Intro verse |
| 15–19 | 25–32% | **Stakes + inciting problem** (villain intro or a disaster) | Verse |
| 19–26 | 32–43% | **Build:** suit-up ECUs (5–7 s) + 2 teases of the reveal + witness looks up | Quiet build |
| 26–27 | 43–45% | Dip / cue | Cut dip or SFX dip |
| **27** | **45%** | **DROP: main reveal** | **Drop** |
| 27–36 | 45–60% | **Ladder rung 1:** first feat + witness reaction ECU | Loud section |
| 36–45 | 60–75% | **Ladder rung 2:** a bigger feat (height, a vehicle, water) | Loud section |
| **45–51** | **75–85%** | **Ladder rung 3 / climax** on the chorus downbeat; slow-mo impact | **Second hit** |
| 51–57 | 85–95% | Payoff of the plant (same framing) + humiliated or sincere button | Chorus |
| 57–60 | 95–100% | Stare to lens, or the title last (teaser), or "Coming Soon" | Tail; ident ≤ 6 s (10%) |

- **Shots:** 25–40. **Average:** 1.5–2 s. **Gens:** 10–14 coverage, or 8–9 beat clips of 7 s.
- **Teaser variant** (a new hero, "Episode 0"): ritual → feat → witness → second feat → stare → **title held to the last 5 s**.
- **Letterbox:** off by default.
- **Tail:** ≤ 10% of runtime. A long tail (black card + extra tag) hurt a re-cut test in the reference series; it was removed in the version that performed 12× better.

## 6. D: 90–120 s episode (the sweet spot)

The proven sweet spot: the top three episodes of the reference series ran 86–106 s.

**105 s beat sheet** (scale the seconds; keep the percentages)

| Time | % | Beat | Song section |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–4 | 0–4% | Cold open + title over the opening shot | Soft intro |
| 4–8 | 4–8% | **Hook gag:** a ritual in costume | Intro |
| 8–12 | 8–11% | **Stakes insert** | Intro |
| 12–22 | 11–21% | **Inciting incident:** villain intro (`story-and-series.md` §5) + victim | Verse |
| 22–32 | 21–31% | **Build:** suit-up montage (8–10 s of ECUs: knot, mask, cape, birds burst off a roof) | Quiet build |
| 32–35.5 | 31–34% | **Toy tease parts 1–2** (eye ECU, body in god-rays); dip | Build → dip |
| **35.5** | **34%** | **DROP: the toy or hero reveal (part 3)** | **Drop** |
| 36–55 | 34–52% | **Rung 1:** chase on the ground with 3–4 village tableaux (1 s each) + villain intercuts | Loud verse |
| 55–64 | 52–61% | **Rung 2:** the catch + a 4-shot reaction chain (0.5 s each) | Chorus begins |
| **64** | **61%** | **Second reveal** (an ally, a twist, a power) on the chorus | **Chorus hit** |
| 64–80 | 61–76% | **Rung 3:** the stunt (height, water, a leap between vehicles) | Chorus |
| 80–97 | 76–92% | **Rung 4 / climax fight** in short CU exchanges | Chorus |
| 97–105 | 92–100% | **Sincere payoff** (villain roped, victim home, extreme wide, fade) **or** ident ≤ 10% | Outro |

- **Shots:** 45–66. **Average:** 1.5–2.1 s. **Gens:** 18–24 (coverage).
- **Music:** one master edit of the signature track. Optional **diegetic song switch** at about 50% for the climax (a radio, a wedding band, a phone).
- **Ending:** a rescue episode ends **sincere** (the top episode had no ident and no end card). A gag episode can end on the ident.

## 7. E: 150–200 s event (song change, offer parts)

**First, offer to split.** Past about 160 s, say: "This works better as two parts of 90–120 s, Part 1 ending on the midpoint cliffhanger. Want that?" Make the single long film only on a clear yes.

**156 s beat sheet**

| Time | % | Beat | Music section |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–8 | 0–5% | Hero assembly in ECU fragments + title | Signature track, intro |
| 8–28 | 5–18% | Cold comic hook / chase | Signature |
| 28–41 | 18–26% | Inciting event in macro inserts; SFX carry it | Signature recedes |
| 41–51 | 26–33% | Disaster / loss | Low |
| **51** | **33%** | **TURN + SONG CHANGE** (diegetic if you can) | **Track 2** (sad classic or action) |
| 51–66 | 33–42% | Discovery; a dip on the key object | Track 2 |
| 66–101 | 42–65% | **Second act:** a flashback (new grade + "N Years Ago" caption), training, or a rematch | Track 2 or score |
| 101–118 | 65–76% | **Build:** the retaliation journey / suit-up | Build |
| 118–120 | 76–77% | **Silence 1–2 s** | Silence |
| **120** | **77%** | **CLIMAX REVEAL:** the power or the climax (`shot-grammar.md` §5) | **Signature returns** (loudest) |
| 120–140 | 77–90% | **Top rungs of the ladder:** a one-take brawl (10–14 s) then 0.6 s KO bursts | Signature chorus |
| 140–156 | 90–100% | Payoff + **cliffhanger** (versus card, "To Be Continued", a post-title stinger) | Title sting |

- **Shots:** 55–80. **Median ≤ 2 s**; at most about **6 holds over 4 s**. An event episode with 15 long holds and one song for 193 s drew "slow even at 2×" comments.
- **Gens:** 25–35; the one-take brawl needs a 10–15 s gen (or two 7 s takes joined by a hidden whip-pan cut).
- **Music:** **2–3 sections, never one song for 150 s+.** Signature → second track → silence → signature for the ending.
- **Letterbox:** allowed for the whole event (it signals "a movie"); drop it for product shots.

## 8. Required slots in every format

| Slot | A | B | C | D | E |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Cold hook in 0–3 s** (works without prior episodes) | frame 0 | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Build before the drop** (suit-up ECUs + reveal teases) | the calm | 2–3 s | 5–7 s | 8–13 s | 15 s + silence |
| **Drop = main reveal** | 40% | 33–40% | 40–50% | 33–38% | 33% turn, 77% climax |
| **Escalation ladder** (each rung bigger in scale or danger: ground → vehicle → height → water/mud/rain → climax) | none | 2 rungs | 3 rungs | 4 rungs | 4–5 rungs |
| **Planted prop + payoff** (same framing) | optional | optional | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Button** (held, readable in 1 s) | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | cliffhanger |
| **New toy / new thing** | it *is* the clip | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Tail / ident** | none | ≤ 3 s | ≤ 6 s | ≤ 10% | ≤ 10% |

**Desi density:** at least **10 named, specific local objects per 60 s** (lota, charpai, hand pump, ration-shop scale, tin trunk…), spread over tableaux. Spend prompt words on objects and places, not on grade adjectives.

## 9. Tie-breaks and edge cases

| Situation | Do this |
|---|---|
| A length and a verb disagree ("a 60 s episode") | **Length wins.** Make format C and say so in one line ("60 s = our vignette format; full episodes run 90–120 s") |
| "A 5 s episode" | Confirm: "a single beat, not a story" → format A |
| Length falls between formats (35 s, 75 s) | Use the shorter format's structure and stretch the ladder by one rung |
| Over 200 s, or a pasted 20-scene story | Keep the user's beats, map them onto these slots, list what was merged or cut, and split into parts of 90–120 s, each ending on a cliffhanger |
| "A fight scene" | 15–30 s = 3–5 gens (`shot-grammar.md` §6) |
| "A trailer" | B or C structure, but withhold the payoff: 3 money shots, the title at the end + "Coming Soon" |
| Budget is tight | Beat-clip mode, fewer rungs, the same location plate reused |
